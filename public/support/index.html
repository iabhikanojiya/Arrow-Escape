Bugs
  • Please include: steps to reproduce (what you did, in order), expected vs. actual result, device model, Android version, app version, and a screen recording or screenshot if possible.
  • Crash? Tell us what was on screen right before it closed.
Gameplay issues / Game not loading / stuck level
  • Try: close the app fully and reopen; restart your device; clear cache (Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape → Storage → Clear cache).
  • If a specific level is stuck, tell us the level number and what you tapped last. A screenshot helps.
  • Include: device model, Android version, app version.
Ads not loading
  • Check your internet connection and try again in a minute. Ad availability depends on region and fill rate — sometimes no ad is available.
  • Make sure battery saver / VPN / ad blockers aren't blocking ads.
  • Include: what happened (banner vs. rewarded), your region (optional), and whether it happens every time.
Rewarded ad rewards not being received
  • Rewards are granted after the ad completes. If you closed early or lost connection, the reward may not register.
  • Wait a few seconds, then check coins/lives. If still missing, email us with: approximate time, reward type (hint / extra life), and whether the ad finished playing.
  • We review rewarded ad logs where available and will help as best we can.
Hint issues / coins missing
  • Hints cost coins (e.g., 40). If coins didn't deduct or a hint didn't appear, restart the level and try once more.
  • If you used a rewarded ad for a hint, see "Reward not received" above.
  • Include: level number, coins before/after (if you remember), and what you tapped.
Lost progress (reinstall / new phone)
  • Progress (levels, coins, lives) is currently stored locally on your device. Clearing app data, reinstalling, or switching devices can reset it.
  • We don't store your progress on a server yet. If cloud save is added later, it will be described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Tip: before reinstalling, make sure Google Play Games / backup (if supported in a future update) is signed in.
Feedback
