How to Play

Three moves. One perfect order.

Arrows can only travel along their valid paths. If another arrow or block is in the way, they stay put. Your job is to find the sequence that lets every arrow escape.

1

Find the right arrow

Look for arrows with a clear exit. Not every arrow can move right away.

Tip: start with arrows that face an open edge.

2

Clear its path

Each arrow slides along its lane until it exits. Nothing teleports — movement is smooth and readable.

Paths are always visible — plan two moves ahead.

3

Escape every arrow

Solve the puzzle in the correct order and the board clears. One wrong move can block you.

Stuck? Use a hint — or come back with fresh eyes.

Coins → Hints

Hint system

  • Use coins to reveal a hint for the current puzzle.
  • If you don't have enough coins, you may optionally watch a rewarded ad to receive a free hint — only when an ad is available.
  • Rewarded ads are always opt-in. You can also keep playing and earn more coins.

We don't use misleading phrases like "free money" or "guaranteed rewards."

♥ Lives & Rewards

Lives and rewards

  • You have 3 lives. A blocked or wrong arrow action can consume a life.
  • When lives run out, restart the level. When available, you may optionally watch a rewarded ad to receive an additional life.
  • Complete levels to earn coins — use them for hints.

Availability of rewarded ads depends on device, region, and fill rate.
